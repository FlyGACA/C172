07 — Kinematic twin (2026-11-06):
- New deterministic model builder (Python + numpy): every part on its own pivot, PBR materials, colour-coded system plumbing, 1:1 airframe envelope from POH Fig 1-1.
- Lycoming cylinder numbering and firing order 1-3-2-4; crank-driven pistons, rods, valves and camshaft; one dual magneto wired to the POH plug map.
- Documented systems simulation (engine/prop, mixture, carb heat and ice, magnetos, starter/priming/flooding, fuel, 28 V electrical, vacuum, ISA pitot-static) with POH-page references.
- 10 guided POH checklist scenarios with live step checking, 14-gauge panel, bilingual Arabic/English UI, offline three.js.
- 41 unit/scenario tests, Playwright browser test, GitHub Actions CI.
- Fixes Phase 6 issues: exploded view had no effect, cylinders 1 and 2 on the same side, two separate magnetos, missing airframe GLB, viewer never browser-tested.

08 — Realistic digital twin (2026-11-06):
- Model built in Blender 4.5 LTS by a Python package (`blender/c172`): subdivision-surface fuselage with boolean window/door openings and hinged doors, NACA 2412/0012 lofted wings with flaps/ailerons, empennage with elevator/rudder/trim tab, tubular gear with fairings, cowl with inlets and landing light, spinner/propeller.
- Full cockpit: 15 instruments with faces drawn from the POH-referenced scales, radio stack, switches, push-pull controls, yokes, pedals, trim wheel, fuel selector, seats and belts.
- O-320-H2AD assembly: finned barrels and heads, accessories, exhaust/muffler, mount, baffles; crank train animated with the Phase 7 kinematics; POH plug map on the plugs.
- Generated PBR textures (livery, rivets/seams, wear, dials, placards), generated sky HDR; editable `.blend`; high/medium/low Draco GLBs with per-level textures.
- New three.js viewer: HDR environment, shadows, GTAO, modes (exterior/cockpit/engine/systems), select/isolate/hide, exploded assembly, animated controls and surfaces, 3D instruments driven by the Phase 7 simulation, 69-entry bilingual part catalogue.
- Tests: Python profile tests, node tests (GLB ↔ kinematics ↔ plug map), GLB validator, Playwright browser test, CI job that rebuilds the model in Blender and compares it.
- Every dimension carries a status (POH page / type data / estimated); geometry remains representative, not manufacturer drawings.

09 — Book figures (2026-11-10):
- Figure plan (`09_book_figures/PLAN.md`): photographs for recognition, vector diagrams for understanding, the 3D twin as a technical-illustration engine; why the generated airframe does not read as a 172 (estimated shape tables) and how to correct it from reference photographs of the real aircraft.
- Figure build: one module per figure drawn as SVG in Chromium, exported as vector PDF (exact size, fonts embedded), SVG and review PNG, in English and Arabic from the same source; reproducible output.
- Illustration renderer for the Phase 8 model (flat tones, ink outlines, tinted subject) that also exports label anchor points, and automatic callout placement.
- Three draft samples: airspeed indicator markings (from `instruments.json`), fuel system schematic, flight control surfaces. Not yet reviewed against the aircraft's POH.
- Tests: manifest, bilingual text, POH provenance keys, agreement between instrument data, simulation constants and the POH table.

08 — Outline corrected, and 09 — illustrations redrawn (2026-11-10):
- The Phase 8 airframe did not read as a Cessna 172. Its outline now follows a side-on photograph of the type, scaled by the POH length (`08_realistic_digital_twin/docs/05_shape_reference.md`): stance and propeller clearance, cowl and nose cap, fuselage lines, cabin section, windows, wrap-around windshield and rear window, swept fin with dorsal fin, raked rudder, tail position, wing struts, landing gear and wheel fairings. Overlap of the model's silhouette with the photograph: 0.78 before, 0.90 now. Status remains `estimated` (the type, not a survey of the FlyGACA aircraft).
- Section tables are interpolated with a shape-preserving cubic instead of an ease curve (no more kinks), the fuselage ends stay square under subdivision (no gap behind the cowl), doors have a gap all round and trim panels inside.
- The geometry validator now measures the built scene against the published span, length, wheel base, track, tail span and propeller clearance, checks 14 attachments, and fails if any of 255 interior parts shows through the skin.
- Reference and gallery renders regenerated: the upper cowl is on in every view except the engine views, and the aircraft stands on the apron instead of a blank floor. Paint stripes now continue across the cowl.
- Book illustrations are drawn with a new renderer: soft shading plus ink lines computed from exact part, depth and surface-direction data (heavy outline, medium part boundaries, fine folds; slender parts drawn fine). New sample S-4, major components of the aircraft, in English and Arabic; S-3 redrawn.
- Tests: interpolation and stance (Python), ink-line rules (Python), the new figure and recipe checks (node), and a figure build in CI that fails if any text runs off a figure.

**Status:** Phases 01–06 are stored exactly as delivered (verify with `npm run verify:checksums`). No flight-certified or maintenance-grade simulation is claimed.

08 — Outline from the handbook drawings, and the aircraft under the skin (2026-11-10):
- The outline is now laid over the manufacturer's three-view and cabin figures instead of a photograph (`blender/threeview.py` draws the model to scale): fuselage sections, doors, windows, wing planform, strut, tail and rudder hinge line redrawn; the aircraft stands in its attitude at rest and reproduces every printed POH dimension, with the maximum height and propeller clearance checked together for the nose gear depressed. Flap travel 0-40 degrees; hinged cabin and baggage doors; a headliner at the level of the wing root; a raised baggage floor.
- Detail levels: 0 basic model, 1 under the skin, 2 fasteners. Sixteen packs in `blender/c172/detail/` add 832 parts: wing, fuselage and tail structure; the control runs of Figure 7-1 with every pulley, bellcrank and push-pull rod; flap drive; fuel tanks, pipes, selector valve and vent; brake, pitot-static, vacuum and stall-warning plumbing; contactors, bus and wiring; rivet rows, screws and bolts; a kit of standard hardware at real size. Each part says what is from the POH and what is representative.
- Inside the engine: tappets, push rods, rocker arms and shafts, valve springs, piston pins and rings, timing gears; the oil system of p.7-18 (pump, suction screen, bypass and relief valves, cooler on the lower right side of the firewall with its hoses, breather, gage line); the inside of the carburetor of p.7-19 (venturi, throttle valve, nozzle, float, mixture valve, accelerator pump) and the carburetor heat valve; the three engine control cables. The rocker boxes of the basic model now stand above the cylinder axis, where the push rods come in.
- Inside the instruments: the airspeed indicator, altimeter, rate-of-climb indicator, attitude indicator, directional indicator, turn coordinator and tachometer each have a case and the mechanism of an instrument of their kind (capsules, linkage, sector and pinion, gimbals and rotor, calibrated leak, magnet and drag cup). The pitot, static and vacuum lines end at the ports on the cases. These show the principle, not the make installed.
- One table (`detail/lanes.py`) lays out where every cable, pipe and wire runs; the structure gets an opening wherever a run passes it. The validator keeps every part inside the wing or fuselage skin, and checks every pipe, hose, cable and wire against every other part: a run may touch what it joins and nothing else. The first run of that check found 68 places where a run cut through another run, an engine mount tube, a stringer or a cylinder; all are rerouted, and the door posts are now channels in which the runs lie side by side.
- Any part on its own: `09_book_figures/blender/part.py` lists, draws (alone, in the phantom aircraft, or with chosen parts as a phantom: `--ghost`) and exports any part, pattern, assembly (`@fuel_system`) or system. A parts index with English and Arabic names (`docs/06_parts_index.md`) and an atlas of every assembly (`09_book_figures/atlas/`).
- Cabin: the heating, ventilating and defrosting system of Figure 7-8 (heat hose from the muffler shroud, heater valve, cabin manifold, defroster duct and outlet, rear ducts, ventilating air door, the two control cables); the overhead console with the flood and dome lights, courtesy lights, door-post map light; door hinges; navigation and transponder antennas.
- Seats where the handbook puts them: the pilot and front passenger at station 37, the rear passengers at station 73, baggage area 1 from station 82 (Figure 6-3). They stood 14 to 16 in too far aft. The pilot's-eye camera of the viewer and of the renders moved with the seat. The rear seat has two seat backs (p.7-11).
- The cabin as the handbook describes it (`detail/cabin.py`): seat rollers, seat lock with its tubular handle, seat back levers, seat stops (p.7-11); seat belt anchors and harness stowage sheaths (p.7-12); door latch, key lock on the left door, door stop, the openable window with hinge, latch and retaining arm (p.7-15); baggage net and tie-down rings (p.7-11); the control lock (p.7-16, detail level 2: fitted only on the ground); light rheostats, lighter, map compartment, microphone, jacks, keying switch, speaker (Figure 7-2, p.7-35 to 7-38).
- The two adjustable ventilators near the upper corners of the windshield, each with its duct from the inlet in the wing root leading edge (p.7-31); the muffler shroud is a part of its own.
- Avionics installation (`detail/avionics.py`): the unit behind each face of the radio stack, the avionics bus and its feed, the antenna cables (communication, navigation, ADF sense, transponder), the speaker wire, the emergency locator transmitter; the ground service plug receptacle (p.7-27). Ground equipment at level 2 (`detail/ground.py`): rudder gust lock, pitot tube cover, tie-down ropes, tow bar, fuel sampler cup, beside the control lock (p.4-4 to 4-6, 7-9, 7-16).
- Web models: high carries level 1 (924 parts, 508 k triangles, 6.5 MB); medium and low stay basic (342 parts).

10 — Flight (2026-11-10):
- Flight model (`shared/js/flight.js`): rigid-body equations in six degrees of freedom with a coefficient model of the wing, tail, propeller and landing gear. Each of its 83 numbers is labelled POH (12), POH-derived (16) or estimated (55), with the handbook page; the list is in the viewer and in `08_realistic_digital_twin/docs/08_flight_model.md`. No certified fidelity.
- Fitted to the handbook: stall speeds of Figure 5-3, static RPM, rate of climb of Figures 5-5 and 5-6, cruise of Figure 5-7, maximum glide, takeoff of Figure 5-4, landing of Figure 5-10. The tests state the tolerances; the document shows the model beside the handbook, computed from the code. Known departure: above 8000 ft full throttle is 4 to 7 knots short of the fastest cruise lines, because the handbook's climb and cruise tables do not agree on the power left at altitude and the model follows the climb table.
- Scripted pilot (`shared/js/pilot.js`): holds attitude, speed, climb, heading and the ball; flies the handbook's short-field takeoff and landing and a power-off stall with its recovery.
- With the systems simulation (`shared/js/flightdeck.js`): the Phase 7 engine turns the propeller, the motion is what the instruments indicate, and a fault acts on the flight. Scenarios that do not fly are unchanged.
- Flight mode of the Phase 8 viewer: runway, cruise or approach; keys or an on-screen wheel; a rudder assistant that can be switched off to show the left-turning tendency; outside and pilot's-eye cameras; weight, centre of gravity, wind and temperature; the turn coordinator's ball and the stall warning; a propeller disc. The 2D gauges of the Phase 8 viewer are styled (they had lost their style sheet).
- Flight lessons (`shared/js/lessons.js`), in the scenario list of the Phase 8 viewer: stall and recovery (p.4-17, 5-11), go-around (p.4-9, 4-20), engine failure after takeoff (p.3-4, 3-11). Steps checked live against the flight; a record of how the lesson was flown; a demonstration by the scripted pilot. New fault on the fault panel: engine failure. The electric flaps need power on the bus. Tests fly each demonstration and the mistakes the handbook warns of (the turn back does not reach the runway).
