# Open data behind the pictures of Thumamah, Riyadh and the peninsula

`../build_oeth_plan.py` builds `../oeth_plan.json`, the ground plan of Thumamah (OETH), from the Sentinel-2 files here and from the AIP values in `../saudi_airspace.json`. `../../figs/terrain_real.py` reads the elevation file for the real ground of Figures 17-1, 17-8 and 17-9. `../../figs/imagery_real.py` lays the picture of the country under the map of Figure 18-8, and `../build_oeth_area.py` reads the highway west of the aerodrome off it into `../oeth_area.json`. Not for navigation.

To refresh: `python fetch_oeth_open.py [first date] [last date]`, then `python ../build_oeth_plan.py --check-picture` (needs numpy, pillow, scikit-image and shapely), look at `oeth_plan_check.png`, and rebuild the plates that draw the plan (18-1, 18-2, 18-7, 26-1). For the picture of the country: `python fetch_oeth_area_open.py`, then `python ../build_oeth_area.py --check-picture` (needs numpy, pillow and scikit-image), look at `oeth_area_check.jpg`, delete `../../renders/terrain-18-08-picture.png` (it is made once) and rebuild Figure 18-8. For the picture of the Red Sea coast: `python fetch_oers_area_open.py`, delete `../../renders/terrain-17-02-coast.png` and rebuild Figure 17-2. For the relief of the peninsula and the globe: `python fetch_arabia_open.py`, delete `../../renders/terrain-20-02-chart.png` and `terrain-20-02-globe.png`, and rebuild Figure 20-2.

The elevation data agree with the AIP where the two can be compared: 1 877 ft at the Thumamah reference point (the AIP's elevation is 1 870 ft) and 1 867 and 1 854 ft at the two thresholds (1 870 and 1 868 ft). It is a radar survey flown in 2000, good to some tens of feet: it shows the shape of the country, not obstacles.
